# Fiber Link Failure Caused by Contaminated LC Connector

## Environment

Residential network / Fiber uplink

## Symptoms

* Fiber link was down.
* No communication between devices.
* Physical connections appeared correct.

## Troubleshooting

* Verified fiber patching.
* Inspected the LC connectors.
* Cleaned the fiber connectors and couplers.
* Re-tested the fiber link.

## Root Cause

A contaminated LC fiber connector (hair/debris inside the coupler) prevented proper optical transmission.

## Solution

Cleaned the LC connector and coupler, reconnected the fiber, and verified the link was restored.

## Lessons Learned

* Always inspect and clean fiber before replacing hardware.
* Even a tiny contaminant can cause complete link failure.
* Keep fiber cleaning tools available during every installation or service call.
