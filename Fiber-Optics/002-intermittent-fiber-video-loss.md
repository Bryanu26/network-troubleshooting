# Intermittent Fiber Video Loss Resolved by Cleaning Fiber Connectors

## Environment

High-end residential AV system featuring a Sony PTZ camera transmitting over fiber to a video processor and a large multi-view display. The display simultaneously presented multiple sources, including live TV, stock market data, and an ocean camera feed.

## Symptoms

* The ocean camera feed would randomly disconnect and later recover on its own.
* Other video sources remained operational.
* The homeowner's caretaker reported recurring outages over an extended period.
* The issue was intermittent and could not be reproduced on demand.

## Troubleshooting

* Replaced the fiber HDMI/video balun.
* Verified fiber patch cables.
* Performed multiple service visits over several weeks.
* Tested the fiber link and confirmed acceptable test results.
* Inspected equipment for hardware faults.
* Returned for a final troubleshooting session with a manager.
* Thoroughly cleaned every fiber connector and coupler in the signal path before reconnecting the system.

## Root Cause

Contamination on one or more fiber connectors caused intermittent optical signal degradation. Although the fiber link passed basic testing, the dirty connectors introduced enough loss to cause random video dropouts.

## Solution

Cleaned all fiber connector end faces and couplers using proper fiber cleaning tools. After reconnecting the system, the camera feed remained stable and the intermittent video loss was eliminated.

## Lessons Learned

* A fiber link can pass continuity or basic signal tests while still experiencing intermittent performance issues.
* Never assume fiber connectors are clean, even if they appear visually acceptable.
* Clean fiber connectors before replacing expensive hardware.
* Intermittent fiber issues often require patience and a methodical troubleshooting process rather than immediately replacing components.
